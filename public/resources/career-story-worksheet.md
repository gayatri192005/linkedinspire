# Career Story Worksheet

Use this worksheet to describe your experience clearly. You do not need a perfectly linear career path.

## Start with a project or experience
- What were you trying to do?
- Who was the work for?
- What was your role?

## Describe your approach
- What steps did you take?
- What skills, tools, or people helped?
- What changed your understanding?

## Capture evidence
- What can you show or link to?
- What outcome can you verify?
- What information must remain private?

## Connect it to your next step
- What would you do differently next time?
- What skill do you want to practise next?
- How would you explain this experience in two sentences?

## A simple story structure
1. Context: the situation or question.
2. Contribution: the work you personally did.
3. Learning: what you discovered or improved.
4. Next step: where you want to go from here.
