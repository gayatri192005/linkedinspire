LINKEDIN POST-WRITING PROMPTS

Use these prompts to brainstorm. Add only details that are true to your experience and write in your own voice.

1. A small lesson I learned while working on [project] was...
2. I used to think [assumption]. After trying [experience], I now think...
3. A question I wish I had asked earlier about [topic] is...
4. One tool or process that helped me with [task] was...
5. A challenge I met while learning [skill] was... Here is what I tried next.
6. A useful resource I found for [audience] is... It helped me because...
7. A project I am proud of is... My role was... The next thing I want to improve is...
8. Three questions I use when I start learning something new are...
9. Feedback that changed my approach to [work] was...
10. If you are exploring [field], one first step you could try is...

Before publishing:
- Check names, numbers, links, and dates.
- Ask permission before sharing someone else's work or story.
- Remove confidential or private information.
- Make the post useful to the reader, not just impressive.
