# LinkedIn Profile Checklist

Use this checklist as a starting point. Keep every statement accurate and in your own voice.

## First impression
- [ ] Use a clear, current profile photo that you are comfortable sharing publicly.
- [ ] Choose a banner image that supports your professional identity without making unsupported claims.
- [ ] Write a headline that explains your focus, skills, or current learning area.
- [ ] Check that your name and profile URL are easy to read and share.

## About section
- [ ] Explain what you are interested in or working toward.
- [ ] Include a specific example, project, or experience when relevant.
- [ ] Use plain language and short paragraphs.
- [ ] Add a clear invitation to connect or learn more.

## Experience and projects
- [ ] Describe your own contribution, not just the team outcome.
- [ ] Add context, actions, tools, and results you can verify.
- [ ] Remove confidential details and information shared without permission.
- [ ] Keep dates, titles, and links up to date.

## Skills and finishing checks
- [ ] Select skills that reflect work you can discuss confidently.
- [ ] Proofread names, dates, links, and spelling.
- [ ] Preview your profile on a phone-sized screen.
- [ ] Revisit the profile when your focus or experience changes.
