# Networking Conversation Starters

Use these examples as a base and make the reason for each message specific. Respect a person's time and choice not to reply.

## Connection request
> Hi [Name], I came across your work on [specific topic or project]. I am learning about [related area] and appreciated [specific detail]. I would be glad to connect.

## A focused question
> Hi [Name], your explanation of [specific idea] helped me think differently about [topic]. If you have a moment, could I ask one question about [focused question]? No worries if now is not a good time.

## After a session or event
> Thank you for sharing [specific takeaway]. I am going to try [small next step]. I appreciated the practical example.

## Follow-up after advice
> I tried your suggestion about [specific action]. Here is what I noticed: [brief result or question]. Thanks again for taking the time to share it.

## Good practice
- Keep the message concise and personal.
- Ask one clear question rather than requesting a large favour immediately.
- Do not send repeated follow-ups if there is no response.
- Never assume a connection will provide a referral or opportunity.
- Share private conversations only with permission.
