# Personal Branding Starter Guide

A personal brand is the impression people form from the work, ideas, and interactions they can actually see. This worksheet helps you make that impression clearer without pretending to be someone else.

## 1. Choose a focus
Write one or two areas you are learning, practising, or contributing to:

- My current focus:
- The people or problems I care about:
- One skill I want to strengthen:

## 2. Collect evidence
List projects, coursework, volunteering, or work that supports your focus. Be specific about your contribution and avoid sharing confidential details.

- Project or experience:
- What I did:
- What I learned:
- Evidence or link I can share:

## 3. Find a useful point of view
What do you notice, question, or explain in a way that could help someone else? A point of view can be a question you are exploring; it does not need to be a bold claim.

## 4. Keep your profile consistent
- Use a headline that reflects your real work or learning.
- Make your About section sound like a clear introduction.
- Share examples that match the topics you want to be known for.
- Review your profile when your goals change.

## 5. Make a small next step
Choose one profile section to improve, one person to learn from, and one idea to write down this week. Consistency is more useful than trying to perfect everything at once.
