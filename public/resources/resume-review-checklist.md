# Resume Review Checklist

## Clarity
- [ ] Put your most relevant experience and skills where a reader can find them quickly.
- [ ] Use consistent headings, dates, punctuation, and spacing.
- [ ] Keep bullets concise and begin with clear action verbs.
- [ ] Check that the document is readable on a phone and when printed.

## Evidence
- [ ] Describe your contribution to each project or role.
- [ ] Include outcomes only when you can explain and support them.
- [ ] Add tools or methods when they help show how you worked.
- [ ] Separate individual work from team results.

## Relevance
- [ ] Tailor examples to the role or opportunity.
- [ ] Use keywords naturally and accurately.
- [ ] Remove unrelated details that make the main story harder to see.
- [ ] Include links only when they are current and relevant.

## Final review
- [ ] Verify names, dates, email, phone, and links.
- [ ] Ask a trusted reviewer for feedback.
- [ ] Save a clean PDF copy with a clear filename.
- [ ] Do not include sensitive personal information that is not needed.
